## Supplementary — first image-reviewer's findings, re-checked against the NEW renders (Nov 12)

The first reviewer audited the OLD (Nov 7) board stills. Its headline matches the second reviewer and this checklist: most of Javion's claims NOT confirmed. It also flagged four additional observations. Each was re-checked against the new renders above:

1. **Recurring bent arm (S5/S37/S38/S41/S46, old board).** In the NEW renders the bent-arm-across-chest is GONE in S5 (arms straight down), S37 (arms straight out, gesture), S38 (arms straight out, gesture), S41 (arms extended out, gesture) — the default-pose fix resolved it. It PERSISTS in S46 only, and there it is an INTENTIONAL gesture, not the default: S46's `poseAt` has Jake's arms keyframed to `{a:25, b:-60}` at f=52, exactly when the face switches to 'worried' — hands coming up anxiously, matching the beat. Same gesture in S48 (face 'scared'). Per the rule ("default arm pose straight down UNLESS a gesture is specified"), a gesture IS specified here. Compliant, not a defect. Judgment call for Javion if he still dislikes the look.
2. **Hanging clock (party scenes).** CONFIRMED present in the new renders: the small round clock hangs from the string-light wire at top centre in S5/S6/S37/S38/S41/S46. It is ATTACHED to the wire (a Party-set piece), not floating — it reads as party decor. Moving it to the wall would be a set-design change, not a bug fix. Not changed; flag for Javion if he wants it wall-mounted.
3. **S5/S37/S46 are the same wide framing.** CONFIRMED: the same party wide recurs at three different beats (different poses, faces, camera zoom each time). They are NOT consecutive shots, so the "no two consecutive shots share a framing" rule is not violated — this is normal scene coverage (returning to the wide), not duplicated props. Not changed; flag for Javion if he wants the wides varied.
4. **S4 mascot detached claw/feet; S6 plant occluded.** S4 is a LOGO/SIGN (BigLobster), not a room scene — the claw/feet separation and the white thumbs-up hand are the sign's flat design, on a flat red background with no floor by design. Not a rig defect. S6: Jake is mid-walk (intentional walkPose, feet at floor level); the background plant is partly occluded by the crowd, which is normal depth, not a placement error. Neither changed.
